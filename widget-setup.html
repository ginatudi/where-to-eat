Where to Eat widget

  1. Tap the button above to copy.
  2. Open the Scriptable app and tap + in the top right.
  3. Paste the script, then tap the name at the top and call it Where to Eat.
  4. Go to your home screen, touch and hold an empty spot, tap + and choose Scriptable.
  5. Pick the small or medium size, tap Add Widget, then tap the widget and choose Script, then Where to Eat.

This tiny script loads the real widget from the web each time, so changes I make show up on their own and you never paste again.
